### Ink/Stitch — GPL-3.0-or-later

- Source: https://github.com/inkstitch/inkstitch
- Copyright (C) the Ink/Stitch authors
- Used for: the thread charts in `src/threads/charts/*.json`. They are generated by `scripts/import-palettes.ts` from the GIMP palette files in `palettes/` at commit `d59c9ab1e390285a6c67822436ffd9ba9843d8b4`. The colour data was converted to JSON. Exact duplicate entries were removed, and the RAL palette (a paint standard) was not included.
- Ink/Stitch's stitch algorithms (fill routing, satin, underlay) were also used as references for the stitch engine.
- The routing benchmark compares the unmodified `find_path` and `_get_shortest_path` functions from `lib/stitches/utils/autoroute.py` at commit `d59c9ab1e390285a6c67822436ffd9ba9843d8b4` (Copyright (c) 2010 Authors, GPL-3.0-or-later). The comparison script loads a separately downloaded upstream source file; it does not bundle that source. LumaStitch's new postman, matching and Euler-walk implementation is original code. See `docs/routing/README.md` for the comparison scope and reproduction steps.
- Brand names and thread codes are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify thread colours.

### Bidirectional layout and Unicode data

- `src/lettering/vendor/bidi` derives from [bidi-js](https://github.com/lojjic/bidi-js), npm version 1.1.0, Copyright 2021 Jason Johnston, MIT. The full license is retained as `LICENSE.txt` in that directory. LumaStitch updates the data to Unicode 17, indexes complete Unicode scalars, and corrects local-line indexing during whitespace reordering. Original source formatting is retained.
- Unicode 17.0.0 bidi classes, paired brackets, mirroring, Scripts and Script_Extensions are generated from the [official Unicode Character Database](https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/). Copyright © 1991–2025 Unicode, Inc.; covered by the Unicode License V3, reproduced as `src/lettering/vendor/bidi/UNICODE-LICENSE.txt`. `src/lettering/unicode17.json` and the representative conformance fixtures derive from the same data. `scripts/generate-lettering-unicode.py` reproduces the tables; the complete official bidi tests can be checked with `tests/tools/check_bidi.mjs`.
